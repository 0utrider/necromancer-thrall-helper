# Credits for bundled art

## `thrall.webp`

Fallback token art, used when a thrall or summon type points at art from a token pack that isn't
installed (for example the Paizo token modules `pf2e-tokens-bestiaries`, `pf2e-tokens-myth-and-magic`
and `pf2e-tokens-monster-core`, or `pf2e-summons-assistant`).

- **Image:** the "soulless" undead portrait from *The Battle for Wesnoth*
  (`data/core/images/portraits/undead/soulless.webp`)
  <https://github.com/wesnoth/wesnoth/blob/master/data/core/images/portraits/undead/soulless.webp>
- **Artist:** doofus-01 (the artist credit stored in the image's EXIF data)

  Battle for Wesnoth lists its art as GPL v2 or later / CC BY-SA 4.0:
  <https://wiki.wesnoth.org/Wesnoth:Copyrights>
